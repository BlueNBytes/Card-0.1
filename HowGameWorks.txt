there are 2 types of cards
each player starts with a 5-card hand and draws a replacement from their 20-card deck after each turn

counters:
have fixed numerical value, can be placed
 on empty cells or cells occupied by
  opponent if their numerical value is lower .

Activecards:
dont have a numerical value holds a description of what they do
they effect gameplay by casting effects eg . 
- doubling the value of the cell you place it on
- placing 2 counters

how to win:
- get a tic tac toe 
- if all cards are used the player with the most cells occupied wins
