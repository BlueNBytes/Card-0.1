how to play?

Here are the basic rules for playing Card 0.1:

  • Players take turns placing cards on the board.
  • The goal is to create a line of three matching cards horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Special cards can be played to gain advantages or hinder opponents.
  • The game continues until one player achieves the winning condition or the board is full.
